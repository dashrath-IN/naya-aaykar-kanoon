Basis: the Income-tax Act, 2025 came into force on 1 April 2026. The 1961 Act stands repealed, but under the savings clause (s.536 of the 2025 Act) it continues to govern tax years beginning before that date and proceedings initiated under it. For TDS, amounts paid or credited on or before 31-3-2026 follow the 1961 Act; amounts paid or credited on or after 1-4-2026 follow the 2025 Act.

Limitations

  • This is a navigation aid, not legal advice. Before citing a provision in a return, reply or opinion, verify it against the official bare Act and the CBDT mapping utility.
  • Subject lines are short descriptions, not the statutory headings verbatim.
  • A 2025 section often maps to several 1961 sections, or one 1961 section is split across several new sections. All links are shown, but sub-clause-level mapping (for example, the clauses of s.10 now in Schedules II–VII, or the definitions in s.2) is not covered.
  • Section 107 of the 2025 Act and the Schedules are not in the sourced tables and are not mapped here.
  • Section 532 (power to frame schemes) consolidates many scheme-making powers. The source abbreviates part of that list.
  • Provisions marked "Finance Act 2026" were substituted, amended or omitted by that Act, per the source.
